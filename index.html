iosPro Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how iosPro (the “App”), an Android home-screen launcher published by SIXAR STUDIOS (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies only to the App.

The short version. The App works on your device. We do not run a server for it, we do not have user accounts, we do not show ads, we do not run any analytics or crash-reporting of our own, and we do not sell your data. The only information that leaves your device is (1) your approximate coordinates, sent to a weather service when you use the Weather widget, (2) purchase handling by Google Play, and (3) the technical data Google may collect through Google Play services and ML Kit (see section 4).

2. Information the App accesses

The App asks for permissions only for features you use. Each permission is optional unless noted, and you can turn any of them off in Android Settings at any time; the related feature will then stop working but the rest of the App continues to work.

Information / permissionWhy the App uses itLeaves your device?
Location (approximate or precise) Shows local weather and air quality in the Weather widget, and the name of your city. Yes. Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (see section 4). The city name is looked up using your device’s built-in geocoder, which depending on your device may contact the device maker’s or Google’s location service.
Calendar (read only) Shows upcoming events and the date in the Calendar widget. No. Events are read and displayed on your device only.

Camera (flashlight control) Used only to switch the flashlight on and off from Control Center. The App does not take photos or video. No.

3. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally, in its private app storage: your layout and settings, widget choices, any notes or checklist items you type into a widget, a cached weather result, and your licence and trial status (see section 5). This data stays on your device. It is deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. Android backup is turned off for the App, so this data is not copied to cloud backups or other devices.

4. Information shared with third parties

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or marketing. The App communicates with the following parties only:

PartyWhat is sharedPurpose
Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), weather data provider The latitude and longitude used for the forecast, and your IP address as a normal part of any internet request. No name, email, device ID or account is sent. To return the forecast and air-quality data shown in the Weather widget. See Open-Meteo’s terms and privacy information.
Google Play (Google LLC), payments and licensing Purchase and entitlement information handled through Google Play Billing, under your Google account. To process the one-time purchase and confirm that you own it. We never see your payment card details. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
Google Play services / ML Kit (Google LLC), on-device image analysis The wallpaper depth effect uses Google’s ML Kit subject segmentation. It runs on your device; your wallpaper is not uploaded. Google Play services downloads the model, and Google states that ML Kit and Play services may collect limited technical information such as performance metrics and device or app details. To separate the subject from the background of your wallpaper. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy.
Your device’s geocoder (device maker / Google) Your coordinates, to turn them into a city name. Display only. This service is part of your device and is governed by its provider’s policy.

The Weather widget only contacts Open-Meteo when a Weather widget is on your home screen. We may also disclose information if required by law, but because we do not collect your data on any server, we generally have nothing to disclose.

5. Purchase and licence information

The App offers a free trial and a one-time purchase, processed entirely by Google Play. After a purchase, the App asks Google Play whether your account owns it, and keeps a tamper-resistant record of the result on your device so the App can work offline. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

6. How long we keep information

We do not keep any of your information on our own servers. Data stored on your device remains until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. Open-Meteo and Google handle any information they receive under their own policies.

7. Security

Information the App stores stays in the App’s private storage, and licence data is protected with keys held in the Android Keystore. Requests to the weather service use an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Permissions: grant or revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings > Apps > iosPro > Permissions.
  • Special access: notification access, the accessibility service and display-over-other-apps can each be turned off in Android Settings.
  • Remove the Weather feature’s data sharing: remove the Weather widget from your home screen.
  • Delete data: clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App, to remove everything it stores on your device.

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have legal rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold your personal data on any server, there is generally nothing for us to retrieve or delete, but you may contact us at the address above with any request and we will respond.

9. Children

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in some regions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help address it.

10. International use

Open-Meteo and Google may process information in countries other than your own. By using the Weather widget, the wallpaper depth effect or making a purchase you understand your information may be handled there under their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do, and for significant changes we will provide a notice in the App or on the Google Play listing.
